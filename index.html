03 — How I build with AI

AI drives. I steer.

AI is my fastest teammate, not my decision-maker. I frame the problem, direct the agent, check its work, and make the call myself.

01

Plan, then delegate

I write the plan and the acceptance checks before an agent writes any code, and split the work into pieces it can do well.

02

Review everything

I read, run and test what the AI produces. Before scaling EMenu I ran inventory, security and cleanup audits on the codebase this way.

03

Overrule it when it's wrong

EMenu's recommendations first called an external LLM. I audited it and replaced it with deterministic local logic: zero AI cost per order and predictable answers.

04

Ship and measure

Real users decide. I put builds in front of restaurant owners, students and judges, then iterate on what they actually do.
